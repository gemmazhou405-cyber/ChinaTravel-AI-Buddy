## 1. TenPayGo official fact summary

- Tencent announced TenPayGo in Shenzhen on September 24, 2026 for international visitors.
- The Shenzhen government English page says visitors can connect supported international bank cards, overseas wallets, or Apple Pay.
- It can be used in the merchant network that accepts WeChat Pay.
- Card, country, merchant, registration, fee, and feature availability are not guaranteed. Confirm the current app instructions before travel.
- Sources: [Shenzhen Government](https://www.sz.gov.cn/en_szgov/aboutsz/videos/content/post_13000672.html) and [China Government payment guide](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202404/11/content_WS6617c858c6d0868f4e8e5f4d.html).

## 2. NIA 12367 accommodation registration fact summary

- From September 21, 2026, online registration for foreigners staying in non-hotel accommodation became available nationwide.
- Official channels include the NIA government-service platform, NIA 12367 app, and official WeChat or Alipay mini-programs.
- It covers private homes, relatives' or friends' homes, owned homes, and other non-hotel accommodation. Hotels continue to register guests during check-in.
- Complete registration within 24 hours and use in-person support or call 12367 if online handling fails.
- Source: [National Immigration Administration](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html).

## 3. 240-hour transit fact summary

- The current policy covers 57 nationalities and 65 designated entry ports.
- It requires a ticketed route from one country or region through mainland China to a different country or region.
- The 240-hour clock starts at 00:00 on the day after entry, but travelers must stay inside the permitted area.
- Nationality alone is not enough; passport type, route, port, onward ticket, purpose, and permitted area all matter.
- Source: [National Immigration Administration](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html).

## 4. Three useful Reddit answer structures

### Payment question

1. Answer the exact question in the first sentence.
2. Separate confirmed provider facts from personal setup variables.
3. Give two backups: a second wallet plus card/RMB cash.
4. Mention the official source date.
5. Add a ChinaEase link only when the community allows it and disclose affiliation.

### Entry-policy question

1. Ask for nationality, ordinary passport, date, purpose, route, ports, and stay length.
2. Distinguish ordinary visa-free entry from third-country transit and Hainan-only rules.
3. State the likely rule in cautious language.
4. Link the official NIA/MFA source first.
5. Suggest the ChinaEase checker only as a planning aid, with disclosure.

### Accommodation question

1. Identify hotel versus non-hotel stay.
2. Explain who registers and the 24-hour timing.
3. List official online channels and the offline fallback.
4. Correct the “foreign hotel licence” myth without promising every booking will be smooth.
5. Offer a relevant guide only if links are permitted.

## 11. UTM naming standard

- `utm_source`: platform or creator slug, for example `reddit`, `facebook`, `youtube_creatorname`.
- `utm_medium`: `community`, `social`, `creator`, `email`, or `referral`.
- `utm_campaign`: `2026_10_tenpaygo`, `2026_10_12367`, `2026_10_visa_checker`, or `founding20`.
- `utm_content`: stable post or creative ID such as `answer_v1`, `group_post_a`, or `video_desc`.
- Use lowercase ASCII and underscores. Never put an email, username, or other personal data in a UTM.
